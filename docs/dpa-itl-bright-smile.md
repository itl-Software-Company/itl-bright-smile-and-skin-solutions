---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this DPA:

- **"Data Protection Laws"** means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and any applicable subordinate legislation;
- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by the Processor on behalf of the Controller under the Service Agreement;
- **"Processing"** has the meaning given in the UK GDPR;
- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller;
- **"Data Subject"** means any identified or identifiable natural person whose Personal Data is processed under this DPA.

---

## 2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

### 2.1 Subject Matter

This DPA governs the processing of Personal Data by the Processor in connection with the provision of the Bright Smile and Skin Solutions website and associated digital systems (the "Platform"), as described in the Service Agreement and its Appendix A (BRD).

### 2.2 Nature and Purpose of Processing

The Processor shall process Personal Data solely for the purpose of:

- Operating and maintaining the website and associated digital platforms;
- Facilitating online booking integration with Hey Goldie;
- Managing client enquiry and contact form submissions;
- Supporting social media content management and scheduling;
- Enabling Google Analytics and website performance monitoring;
- Processing newsletter sign-ups and marketing communications (where consent is given);
- Supporting future client portal functionality.

### 2.3 Duration

Processing shall continue for the duration of the Service Agreement and for such additional period as is necessary to fulfil the obligations set out in this DPA (including data return and deletion).

---

## 3. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS AND PERSONAL DATA

### 3.1 Data Subjects

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Clients | Adult individuals who book or enquire about beauty and aesthetics treatments |
| Website Visitors | Individuals who browse the website or interact with online content |
| Staff / Therapists | Employees or contractors of No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions (future phase) |
| Business Owner | Jacqie — owner and operator of the business |

> [!NOTE]
> All data subjects are adults. No children's data is processed through this Platform.

### 3.2 Categories of Personal Data

| Category | Examples | Sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| **Client Contact Data** | Name, email address, phone number | Standard |
| **Booking Data** | Appointment date/time, treatment type, booking reference (via Hey Goldie) | Standard |
| **Treatment Records** | Treatment history, aftercare notes, practitioner notes | High (Health-Related) |
| **Before/After Photography** | Photographs of treatment results (with explicit client consent) | High (Biometric-Adjacent) |
| **Financial Records** | Invoice amounts, payment status (no card data stored directly) | Standard |
| **Marketing Consent Records** | Consent status, opt-in date, communication preferences | Standard |
| **Website Analytics Data** | Anonymised IP address, device type, pages visited, session duration | Low (Anonymised) |

### 3.3 Special Category Data

The Processor acknowledges that:

(a) **Treatment records** may contain or reference health-related data and shall be treated with enhanced protections;

(b) **Before/after photography** captures physical appearance data related to health treatments and requires explicit client consent for processing and display.

## 4. LAWFUL BASES FOR PROCESSING

The Controller confirms the following lawful bases under Article 6 of the UK GDPR:

| Purpose | Lawful Basis |
|---|---|
| Service delivery (booking, treatment records) | **Contract performance** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Marketing communications (newsletters, offers) | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Financial record-keeping | **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — HMRC requirements |
| Website analytics (anonymised) | **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Before/after photography (marketing use) | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Staff/therapist records (future) | **Contract performance** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |

---

## 5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONTROLLER

The Controller shall:

5.1. Ensure that it has a lawful basis for processing under Article 6 of the UK GDPR for all Personal Data provided to the Processor;

5.2. Provide clear and transparent privacy notices to all Data Subjects in accordance with Articles 13 and 14 of the UK GDPR;

5.3. Obtain explicit consent from clients for marketing communications and for the use of before/after photography;

5.4. Ensure that Personal Data provided to the Processor is accurate and up to date;

5.5. Be responsible for responding to Data Subject rights requests, with the Processor providing reasonable assistance as set out in clause 8.

---

## 6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROCESSOR

The Processor shall:

6.1. Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do so by law (in which case the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless prohibited by law from doing so);

6.2. Ensure that all personnel authorised to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality;

6.3. Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures as set out in clause 7;

6.4. Not engage a Sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Controller, subject to clause 9;

6.5. Assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligations under Data Protection Laws, including Data Subject rights, data protection impact assessments, and consultation with the ICO;

6.6. Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for audits and inspections, subject to reasonable notice;

6.7. Immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.

---

## 7. SECURITY MEASURES

### 7.1 Technical Measures

The Processor shall implement and maintain the following technical security measures:

| Measure | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Encryption at rest** | All personal data encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent (Google Cloud default encryption) |
| **Encryption in transit** | TLS 1.2+ for all data transmission |
| **Access control** | Role-based access control (RBAC) with data minimisation by role |
| **Authentication** | Secure authentication with enforced password policies |
| **Audit logging** | Timestamped, immutable audit trail for sensitive operations |
| **Backup** | Automated daily backups with encrypted storage |

### 7.2 Organisational Measures

| Measure | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Staff training** | All ITL personnel with platform access receive data protection training |
| **Access reviews** | Quarterly review of access permissions |
| **Incident response** | Documented breach response procedure (see clause 10) |
| **Development practices** | Privacy by design and by default in all development |

---

## 8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

8.1. The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III of the UK GDPR, including:

- Right of access (Article 15)
- Right to rectification (Article 16)
- Right to erasure (Article 17)
- Right to restriction of processing (Article 18)
- Right to data portability (Article 20)
- Right to object (Article 21)

8.2. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay upon receiving a request from a Data Subject directly, and shall not respond to the request without the Controller's instruction unless legally required to do so.

8.3. The Processor shall provide the Controller with the technical means to export, rectify, or delete Personal Data in a standard, portable format.

---

## 9. SUB-PROCESSORS

### 9.1 Authorised Sub-processors

The Controller hereby grants general written authorisation for the Processor to engage the following Sub-processors:

| Sub-processor | Service | Data Processed | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud Platform (GCP) | Cloud hosting and infrastructure | All platform data | UK / EEA |
| Firebase (Google) | Authentication and hosting | User credentials, session data | UK / EEA |

| Hey Goldie | Booking integration | Client name, contact details, appointment data | UK |
| GitHub | Source code hosting and CI/CD | No personal data (code only) | US (SCCs in place) |

### 9.2 Changes to Sub-processors

(a) The Processor shall inform the Controller in writing of any intended changes to Sub-processors, giving the Controller **fourteen (14) days** to object.

(b) If the Controller raises a reasonable objection, the Parties shall discuss the concern in good faith. If no resolution is reached, the Controller may terminate the Service Agreement in accordance with its termination provisions.

### 9.3 Sub-processor Obligations

The Processor shall ensure that any Sub-processor is bound by data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

---

## 10. PERSONAL DATA BREACH

10.1. The Processor shall notify the Controller **without undue delay** and in any event within **24 hours** of becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

10.2. The notification shall include, to the extent available:

(a) A description of the nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;

(b) The name and contact details of the Processor's data protection point of contact;

(c) A description of the likely consequences of the breach;

(d) A description of the measures taken or proposed to address the breach, including measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

10.3. The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller and take reasonable steps to assist in the investigation and remediation of the breach.

10.4. The Processor acknowledges that the Controller is responsible for notifying the ICO within **72 hours** where the breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects, and shall provide all necessary information to enable this.

---

## 11. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

11.1. The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom without the prior written consent of the Controller.

11.2. Where a transfer is necessary (e.g., to a Sub-processor located outside the UK), the Processor shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

- UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA); or
- UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

11.3. The Processor confirms that, as of the Effective Date, all primary data processing occurs within the **United Kingdom** or **European Economic Area** on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure.

---

## 12. DATA RETURN AND DELETION

12.1. Upon termination or expiry of the Service Agreement, the Processor shall, at the Controller's election:

(a) Return all Personal Data to the Controller in a standard, machine-readable format (e.g., CSV, JSON); or

(b) Securely delete all Personal Data and provide written confirmation of such deletion.

12.2. The Processor shall complete the return or deletion within **thirty (30) days** of termination.

12.3. The Processor may retain Personal Data in backup systems for a further period not exceeding **ninety (90) days**, after which it shall be permanently deleted.

12.4. The Processor may retain anonymised or aggregated data (from which no individual can be identified) indefinitely for statistical or analytical purposes.

---

## 13. AUDITS AND COMPLIANCE

13.1. The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

13.2. The Controller may conduct audits or inspections of the Processor's data processing activities, subject to:

(a) **Fourteen (14) days' prior written notice**;

(b) The audit being conducted during normal business hours;

(c) The Controller bearing the reasonable costs of the audit.

13.3. The Processor shall cooperate fully with any audit and provide access to relevant systems, records, and personnel.

---

## 14. LIABILITY

14.1. The liability provisions of the Service Agreement (clause 8) shall apply to this DPA.

14.2. Each Party shall be liable for damages caused by processing which infringes Data Protection Laws, to the extent of its respective responsibility under those laws.

---

## 15. TERM AND TERMINATION

15.1. This DPA shall take effect on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for as long as the Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

15.2. This DPA may not be terminated independently of the Service Agreement, except by mutual written agreement.

15.3. Clauses 10 (Breach notification), 12 (Data return/deletion), and 13 (Audits) shall survive termination of this DPA.

---

## 16. GENERAL

16.1. **Conflict**: In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Service Agreement, the terms of this DPA shall prevail in respect of data protection matters.

16.2. **Governing Law**: This DPA shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

16.3. **Jurisdiction**: The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
