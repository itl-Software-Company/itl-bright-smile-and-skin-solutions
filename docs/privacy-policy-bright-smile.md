## 2. WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you:

## 3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

|---|---|---|

|---|---|

| Referral source | Understanding how visitors find us |

## 4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We process personal data for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Lawful Basis (UK GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Confirming and managing your bookings | **Contract performance** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Providing treatments and aftercare advice | **Contract performance** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Keeping treatment records | **Contract performance** and **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)) |
| Sending marketing communications (newsletters, offers) | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — you can opt out at any time |
| Using before/after photos in marketing | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — we will always ask your explicit permission |
| Financial record-keeping | **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — HMRC requirements |
| Website analytics (anonymised) | **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — improving our website |
| Responding to your enquiries | **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

### 4.1 We Do NOT Use Your Data For

- Selling your data to third parties
- Automated decision-making or profiling
- Any purpose beyond what is described above

## 7. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Client treatment records** | 7 years from last treatment | Professional indemnity insurance standard |
| **Before/after photographs** | Duration of your consent + 1 year after withdrawal | Consent-based; allows time for removal from all media |
| **Financial records** | 6 years from date of transaction | HMRC requirements |
| **Booking data** | 2 years after last appointment | Operational purposes and rebooking |
| **Marketing consent records** | Duration of consent + 1 year after withdrawal | Compliance and audit trail |
| **Website analytics data** | 26 months | Google Analytics default retention period |
| **Contact enquiries** | 12 months from enquiry date | Customer service follow-up |

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

## 9. COOKIES

### 9.1 Essential Cookies

Our website uses **strictly necessary cookies** for:

- Session management (keeping you connected during your visit)
- Security tokens (preventing cross-site request forgery)

These cookies are exempt from consent requirements under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

### 9.2 Analytics Cookies

We use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised data about page views, session duration, and traffic sources.

- Google Analytics cookies are set only with your consent
- You can opt out at any time using our cookie preferences or by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Data collected is anonymised (IP anonymisation is enabled)

### 9.3 No Advertising Cookies

We do **not** use:

- Advertising or tracking cookies
- Third-party social media tracking cookies
- Any cookies for behavioural profiling
