## 1. PURPOSE

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into for the purpose of facilitating discussions and the exchange of confidential information regarding the design, development, and deployment of a professional website with integrated SEO, social media content management system, booking integration, and future client portal (the "Project").

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all information, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

- **Technical Information**: Software designs, source code, system architecture, APIs, databases, algorithms, technical specifications, and Business Requirements Documents (BRDs);
- **Business Information**: Business plans, financial data, pricing structures, service portfolios, client lists, customer records, treatment records, before/after photography, operational procedures, and standard operating procedures (SOPs);
- **Personal Data**: Any data relating to identified or identifiable individuals, including clients and staff, as defined under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018;
- **Proprietary Information**: Trade secrets, know-how, inventions, processes, and any other information that is marked as "confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

3.1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;

3.2. Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose described in clause 1;

3.3. Take all reasonable measures to protect the confidentiality of the Confidential Information, using no less than the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information;

3.4. Restrict disclosure of Confidential Information to its employees, agents, and subcontractors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those in this Agreement;

3.5. Immediately notify the Disclosing Party upon discovery of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information shall not include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;

(b) Was rightfully in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure, without restriction on disclosure;

(c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information;

(d) Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure;

(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of such requirement.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION

5.1. Both Parties acknowledge that the exchange of Confidential Information may involve the processing of personal data as defined by the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

5.2. Each Party shall comply with its obligations under applicable data protection legislation in respect of any personal data received under this Agreement.

5.3. Neither Party shall transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom without appropriate safeguards in place.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1. No licence or right is granted under this Agreement to either Party in respect of the other's Confidential Information, except the limited right to use such information for the Purpose.

6.2. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

6.3. ITL retains all intellectual property rights in its platform technology, frameworks, and tools, including the worzl.ai platform. Any bespoke development created specifically for No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions shall be subject to the terms of the separate Service Agreement.

## 7. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION

7.1. Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information in its possession and provide written confirmation of such return or destruction.

7.2. The Receiving Party may retain copies of Confidential Information in archival or backup systems, subject to the continuing obligations of this Agreement, or where required by law.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1. This Agreement shall be effective from the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of **two (2) years** from the date of last disclosure of Confidential Information, unless terminated earlier by either Party with thirty (30) days' written notice.

8.2. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive termination of this Agreement for a further period of **two (2) years**.

## 9. REMEDIES

> **Why this clause exists:** Both Parties will be sharing sensitive information — ITL's proprietary platform technology and No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions' client data, treatment records, pricing strategies, and before/after photography. If either side leaked the other's confidential information, financial compensation alone wouldn't undo the damage (e.g. you can't "un-leak" client records or proprietary code). This clause allows either Party to ask a court to **immediately stop** the breach, rather than waiting to sue for damages after the harm is already done.

9.1. Both Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy.

9.2. In the event of a breach or threatened breach, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek **injunctive relief** (a court order requiring the breaching Party to stop the harmful activity immediately) or other **equitable relief** (any non-monetary remedy the court considers fair), in addition to any other remedies available at law.

9.3. This right applies equally to both Parties and does not limit either Party's right to claim damages.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1. **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to confidentiality and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements relating thereto.

10.2. **Amendment**: This Agreement may not be amended except by written instrument signed by both Parties.

10.3. **No Waiver**: The failure of either Party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that right.

10.4. **Severability**: If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

10.5. **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

10.6. **Jurisdiction**: The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
