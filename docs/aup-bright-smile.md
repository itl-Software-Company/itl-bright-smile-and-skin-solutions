## 1. PURPOSE

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules and responsibilities for all users of the digital platforms provided to No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions, including the website, content management systems, social media tools, and any future client portal (collectively, the "Platform"). By accessing the Platform, you agree to comply with this policy.

This policy applies to all user roles:

- **Owner** (Jacqie — business owner and primary administrator)
- **Staff / Therapists** (employees or contractors with platform access — future phase)
- **Clients** (customers accessing any future client portal — future phase)

---

## 2. YOUR ACCOUNT

2.1. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. Do not share your password with anyone.

2.2. If you suspect your account has been compromised, notify the business owner (Jacqie) immediately.

2.3. You must provide accurate information when setting up your account and keep it up to date.

2.4. Each user must have their own individual account. Shared accounts are not permitted.

---

## 3. ACCEPTABLE USE

You **may** use the Platform to:

### 3.1 Owner / Admin

- Manage and update website content (text, images, treatment information)
- Upload before/after photographs (only with documented client consent)
- Manage social media content through the content approval dashboard
- View and manage booking data integrated from Hey Goldie
- Access website analytics and performance reports
- Manage marketing communications and client consent preferences
- Administer user accounts and access permissions

### 3.2 Staff / Therapists (Future Phase)

- View their own appointment schedule
- Access relevant client treatment notes (within their scope)
- Upload before/after photographs with client consent
- Submit content for social media approval

### 3.3 Clients (Future Portal)

- View their own booking history and upcoming appointments
- Access aftercare information relevant to their treatments
- Update their contact details and communication preferences
- View and manage their marketing consent preferences

---

## 4. PROHIBITED USE

You must **not**:

### 4.1 Security

- Share your login credentials with any other person
- Attempt to access data, accounts, or areas of the Platform outside your assigned role
- Attempt to bypass, disable, or circumvent any security controls
- Use automated tools, bots, or scripts to access the Platform
- Deliberately introduce malware, viruses, or other harmful code

### 4.2 Data and Privacy

- Access, copy, or export personal data for any purpose not authorised by the business owner
- Share client data, treatment records, or before/after photographs with unauthorised persons
- Upload or display before/after photographs without verified client consent
- Use personal data obtained through the Platform for marketing, commercial, or any purpose unrelated to the business
- Take screenshots, photos, or recordings of client data for personal use

### 4.3 Content

- Upload inappropriate, offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content to the website or social media channels
- Publish misleading claims about treatments, results, or qualifications
- Post content that infringes on any third party's intellectual property or privacy rights
- Misrepresent your identity or role on the Platform

### 4.4 Communications

- Send abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or inappropriate messages through any Platform feature
- Use Platform communications for commercial solicitation unrelated to the business
- Share another user's personal information without their consent

---

## 5. CONTENT AND PHOTOGRAPHY RESPONSIBILITIES

### 5.1 Before/After Photography

- **Always** obtain explicit, documented consent from the client before taking before/after photographs
- **Always** obtain separate consent before using photographs for marketing or social media
- Clients may withdraw consent at any time — photographs must be removed from all platforms within a reasonable timeframe
- Never share identifiable photographs without consent, even privately

### 5.2 Social Media Content

- All social media posts should be reviewed through the content approval dashboard before publishing
- Ensure all claims about treatments are accurate, honest, and compliant with Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) guidelines
- Tag and credit appropriately when reposting or using third-party content
- Respond to comments and messages professionally

### 5.3 Website Content

- Keep treatment descriptions, pricing, and availability up to date
- Ensure all imagery used on the website has appropriate permissions or licences
- Remove outdated offers, promotions, or events promptly
