## 1. OVERVIEW

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") sets out the terms under which ITL will provide software development, digital marketing, and consultancy services to the Client for the design, development, and deployment of a professional website with integrated SEO, social media content management, booking integration, and future client portal (the "Project"), as outlined in the Business Requirements Document ("BRD") and delivered across three phases of implementation (see **Appendix A**).

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

### 2.1 Three-Phase Delivery

The Project shall be delivered across three phases, each governed by the BRD:

**Phase 1 — Website & SEO Foundation**
- Professional website redesign and development
- Search engine optimisation (SEO) — local and organic
- Integration with Hey Goldie booking system
- Google Business Profile optimisation
- Google Analytics and performance monitoring setup
- Mobile-responsive, accessible design

**Phase 2 — Social Media & Content System**
- Social media content calendar and scheduling system
- Content approval dashboard
- Multi-platform posting (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- Per-channel analytics and reporting
- Before/after gallery management with consent tracking

**Phase 3 — AI & Automation (Future)**
- AI-powered content generation and suggestions
- Automated review and reputation management
- Client portal for booking history and aftercare
- Advanced analytics and business intelligence

### 2.2 Scope Protection

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Any work not explicitly described in the Appendix A BRD is out of scope for this Agreement.** Requests for additional features, modifications, or changes beyond the BRD scope will be treated as Change Requests under clause 7.

### 2.3 Fixed-Price Delivery

This Agreement provides a **fixed-price delivery** for the scope defined in the BRD for each active phase. The fee covers all necessary development, design, and consultancy required to deliver the documented features, including:

- Discovery and requirements workshops to validate the BRD and map workflows.
- Phased review and feedback sessions across the development term.
- Comprehensive platform build, testing, and deployment.

### 2.4 Meeting Schedule

The development follows a phased cadence across each active phase:

- **Month 1**: Discovery session (1–2 hours) + review meeting
- **Subsequent months**: Monthly review meeting + ongoing development
- **Final month of phase**: Final review meeting + delivery and handover

4.1. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall continue for the duration of the active development phase (the "Development Term").

4.3. The Client may terminate during the Development Term by providing **thirty (30) days' written notice**, subject to payment of fees due for the current month.
