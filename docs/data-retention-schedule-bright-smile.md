# DATA RETENTION & DELETION SCHEDULE

**No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions — Digital Platforms**

**Reference:** ITL-BSSS-DRS-001

**Version:** 1.0

**Date:** 24 June 2026

**Effective Date:** ____________________

> This schedule is a companion document to the Data Processing Agreement (ITL-BSSS-DPA-001) and the Privacy Policy (ITL-BSSS-PP-001). It defines the retention periods for all categories of personal data processed through the website and associated digital platforms.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This Data Retention and Deletion Schedule establishes clear retention periods for all personal data categories processed through the No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions website and digital platforms. It ensures compliance with the UK GDPR's **storage limitation** principle (Article 5(1)(e)) — personal data shall be kept for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.

---

## 2. RETENTION SCHEDULE

### 2.1 Client Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Client contact details** (name, email, phone) | **7 years** from last treatment | Last appointment date | Professional indemnity insurance; rebooking window |
| **Treatment records** (treatment type, practitioner notes, aftercare) | **7 years** from last treatment | Last appointment date | Professional indemnity insurance standard for beauty/aesthetics practitioners |
| **Before/after photographs** | Duration of consent + **1 year** after consent withdrawal | Consent withdrawal date (or last treatment if no withdrawal) | Consent-based processing; 1-year buffer allows removal from all media channels |
| **Booking data** (appointment date/time, treatment booked, booking reference) | **2 years** after last appointment | Last appointment date | Operational purposes, rebooking, trend analysis |
| **Client consultation notes** | **7 years** from last treatment | Last appointment date | Aligned with treatment records; professional indemnity |

### 2.2 Financial Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Invoice records** | **6 years** from invoice date | Invoice date | HMRC requirements (Limitations Act 1980) |
| **Payment records** (status only — no card data stored) | **6 years** from payment date | Payment date | HMRC requirements |
| **Gift voucher records** | **2 years** from issue date or redemption, whichever is later | Issue or redemption date | Operational; consumer protection |

### 2.3 Marketing Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketing consent records** (opt-in date, preferences) | Duration of consent + **1 year** after withdrawal | Consent withdrawal date | PECR compliance; audit trail for consent |
| **Newsletter subscriber data** | Until unsubscribe + **30 days** | Unsubscribe date | Consent-based; processing window for removal |
| **Social media interaction data** | Not retained by us (held by platform) | N/A | Third-party platform policies apply |

### 2.4 Website and Analytics Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Analytics data** | **26 months** from collection | Data collection date | Google Analytics default retention period |
| **Contact form submissions** | **12 months** from submission date | Submission date | Customer service follow-up |
| **Cookie consent preferences** | **12 months** from preference set | Preference date | PECR compliance |

### 2.5 Staff / Therapist Data (Future Phase)

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Staff account profile** (name, email, role) | Duration of employment + **6 months** | Employment end date | HR records; transition period |
| **Staff access logs** | **2 years** from log date | Log creation date | Security and audit trail |

### 2.6 System Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Audit logs** (login, content changes, data access) | **3 years** from log date | Log creation date | Security and compliance |
| **Session data** | Duration of session + **24 hours** | Session end | Security |
| **Backup data** | **90 days** rolling | Backup creation date | Disaster recovery |
| **Error logs** (anonymised) | **12 months** | Log creation date | Technical debugging |

## 3. DELETION PROCEDURES

### 3.1 Automated Deletion

The following data shall be automatically purged by the Platform:

| Data | Automated Action |
|---|---|
| Expired sessions | Deleted after 24 hours |
| Backups older than 90 days | Purged from backup storage |
| Google Analytics data beyond 26 months | Automatically expired by Google |

### 3.2 Scheduled Review Deletion

The following data shall be reviewed and deleted on a **six-monthly basis**:

| Data | Review Action |
|---|---|
| Client records past 7-year retention | Permanently deleted |
| Booking data past 2-year retention | Permanently deleted |
| Contact form submissions past 12 months | Permanently deleted |
| Before/after photos where consent has been withdrawn (past 1-year buffer) | Permanently deleted from all platforms |
| Marketing subscribers who unsubscribed (past 30 days) | Permanently deleted |

### 3.3 Manual Deletion (Data Subject Request)

Upon receiving a valid erasure request under Article 17 of the UK GDPR:

1. The request is logged and acknowledged within **5 working days**
2. Data is assessed for retention obligations (legal holds, HMRC, professional indemnity)
3. Data not subject to legal retention is deleted within **one calendar month**
4. The Data Subject is notified of completion
5. Data held in backups is purged at next backup cycle (within 90 days)

### 3.4 Exceptions to Deletion

Data may be retained beyond the scheduled retention period where:

- Required by law (e.g., HMRC requirements for financial records)
- Required for professional indemnity insurance purposes
- Subject to an ongoing or anticipated legal claim
- Required for compliance with a court order or regulatory investigation
- The Data Subject has given explicit consent to extended retention

---

## 4. ANONYMISATION

Where data is required for statistical or reporting purposes beyond its retention period, it shall be **anonymised** such that no individual can be identified. Anonymised data is no longer personal data under the UK GDPR and may be retained indefinitely.

Examples:
- Aggregate treatment popularity statistics
- Revenue trends by treatment category
- Website traffic patterns
