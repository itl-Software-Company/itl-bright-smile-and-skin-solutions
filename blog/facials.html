Beyond the Basic Facial: Advanced Skincare Treatments That Truly Transform Your Skin

15 February 2026 · 6 min read

Facials have evolved far beyond simple cleansing and moisturising. Today's advanced facial treatments combine cutting-edge technology with clinical-grade ingredients to address a wide range of skin concerns, from acne and hyperpigmentation to fine lines and dullness.

At No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions, our facial menu is designed to offer something for every skin type and concern, from a quick refresh to intensive clinical transformation.

Understanding Your Skin's Needs

Every skin is different, which is why a one-size-fits-all approach to skincare simply does not work. Before any treatment at our clinic, our skincare specialists will assess your skin's condition, discuss your concerns and goals, and recommend a treatment plan tailored to your unique needs. Whether you are dealing with congestion, dehydration, ageing, sensitivity, or uneven texture, there is a treatment in our range that can help.

Our Treatment Range

Our facial offerings span ten distinct treatments, each targeting specific skin concerns. At the premium end, our Skin Booster (£150) delivers intensive hydration and rejuvenation for luminous, youthful skin using advanced serums and techniques. The Deluxe Facial (£80) provides a comprehensive deep-cleansing experience for visible radiance, while the Detox Facial (£70) purifies and refreshes congested skin.

For those seeking hydration, the Hydrofacial (£65) uses a multi-step process to cleanse, exfoliate, and infuse the skin with nourishing serums, leaving it plump and dewy. Our Microneedling treatment (£65) stimulates collagen production by creating controlled micro-injuries in the skin, promoting natural healing and resulting in smoother texture, reduced scarring, and improved overall tone.

The Rejuvenate Facial (£55) focuses on anti-ageing, restoring vitality and youthful glow through targeted serums and massage techniques. For a brightening boost, the Glow Facial (£45) enhances your natural radiance, while the Express Facial (£45) delivers maximum results in minimal time — perfect for busy lifestyles. Dermaplaning (£35) exfoliates the skin and removes fine vellus hair for an incredibly smooth finish, and LED Therapy (£30) uses specific wavelengths of light to stimulate collagen, reduce inflammation, and target acne-causing bacteria.

How Often Should You Have a Facial?

The frequency of your facial treatments depends on your skin concerns and goals. As a general guideline, a professional facial every four to six weeks aligns with the skin's natural cell turnover cycle, allowing you to maintain consistent results. For specific concerns like acne or hyperpigmentation, your practitioner may recommend a more intensive initial course of treatments followed by maintenance sessions.

The Clinical Difference

What sets a clinical facial apart from a high-street or spa treatment is the quality of the products used, the expertise of the practitioner, and the results achieved. At No.1 Bright Smile and Skin Solutions, our treatments use professional-grade formulations that penetrate deeper and deliver more active ingredients than retail products. Our practitioners are trained to identify underlying skin concerns and adjust treatment protocols accordingly, ensuring you receive genuinely effective care rather than a purely relaxing experience.

Investing in your skin is one of the most impactful things you can do for your confidence and wellbeing. Whether you are new to clinical skincare or looking to elevate your existing routine, we invite you to experience the difference at our Newcastle under Lyme clinic.
